WEBSITE INFORMATION

How this website handles enquiries

What happens to the details you enter here, and how to contact us with a question.

Your enquiry stays in the browser until you choose to send it

The form prepares a summary on your device. This website does not submit that summary to a CIASecured server or store it in a client database.

You choose how to send it

Email opens a draft in your email application. WhatsApp opens a draft for the public contact number. Those services handle the information according to their own settings and terms. Nothing is automatically sent by this website.

No form drafts are saved in browser storage

We do not use local storage or cookies to save your enquiry. Copy enquiry writes the summary to your clipboard only when you press that button. Avoid including confidential documents, passwords or sensitive personal information.

Hosting and external links

The public website is hosted on GitHub Pages. The hosting provider may process technical request information. Links to the client portal, email and WhatsApp open separate services. This website does not include advertising analytics or third-party tracking scripts.

Questions about your details

Contact famojuroadeyemi@gmail.com to discuss an enquiry you have sent. The separate client application has its own authenticated workspace.
