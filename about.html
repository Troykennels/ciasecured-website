ABOUT US

About CIASecured

A UK-based implementation company working with organisations internationally on security, privacy and assurance requirements.

OUR ROLE

Helping organisations put requirements into practice

We bring structure to the work between an agreed objective and an operational result. That means defining responsibilities, working through implementation actions and reviewing the evidence before handover.

OUR FOCUS

Practical implementation
with clear accountability

The work should reflect how your organisation operates and leave your people with responsibilities and records they can use.

WORKING PRINCIPLES

What guides an engagement

01

Understand the context

Begin with the organisation, the requirement and the outcome the work needs to support.

02

Make ownership clear

Agree who delivers, who reviews and who makes decisions throughout the programme.

03

Keep the evidence connected

Maintain a useful record of implementation work, supporting evidence and outstanding actions.

04

Prepare for continuity

Plan the handover so your organisation understands what is complete and what comes next.

INTERNATIONAL DELIVERY

Work shaped around your organisation

Locations, time zones, stakeholders and applicable requirements are considered when an engagement is scoped. Delivery arrangements are agreed with your team.
