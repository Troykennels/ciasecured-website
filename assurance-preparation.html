IMPLEMENTATION SERVICES

Assurance preparation

Bring the implementation record together for a clear and informed review.

WHERE THIS HELPS

Prepare to demonstrate the work

An upcoming assessment or customer review can expose missing evidence and unclear ownership. We help organise records and identify work still required.

THE WORK INVOLVED

A scope shaped around your requirements

01

Agree the review scope

Identify the agreed framework, requirements, reviewers and intended use of the evidence.

02

Review the evidence

Connect supporting records to work completed and identify gaps requiring attention.

03

Resolve outstanding actions

Work with named owners to prioritise open points and record decisions made.

04

Prepare for handover

Create a reviewable record of evidence, remaining work and responsibility for the next stage.

PRACTICAL OUTPUTS

What the engagement can provide

Deliverables are confirmed with you before work begins.

  • An evidence review structure
  • A record of gaps and outstanding actions
  • Agreed preparation priorities
  • A documented review and handover record

Agreeing the boundaries

Preparation is separate from independent assessment or certification. External assessor arrangements, fees and outcomes are not included unless explicitly agreed.
