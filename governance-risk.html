IMPLEMENTATION SERVICES

Governance & risk

Give security decisions a clear owner and an agreed foundation.

WHERE THIS HELPS

Establish the right priorities

A new programme, changing requirements or fragmented responsibilities can make it difficult to decide where to begin. We help establish the scope and risk priorities before implementation starts.

THE WORK INVOLVED

A scope shaped around your requirements

01

Understand the organisation

Review objectives, stakeholders, current arrangements and the requirements shaping the programme.

02

Assess and prioritise risk

Identify risks in scope and agree how treatment decisions will be made and recorded.

03

Define responsibility

Set out who owns the programme, controls, decisions and reviews within your organisation.

04

Prepare the delivery plan

Translate findings into prioritised implementation actions, dependencies and review points.

PRACTICAL OUTPUTS

What the engagement can provide

Deliverables are confirmed with you before work begins.

  • An agreed programme scope
  • A record of risks and treatment decisions
  • A responsibility and decision structure
  • A prioritised implementation plan

Agreeing the boundaries

The assessment boundaries and level of detail are agreed during discovery. Risk decisions remain with your organisation.
