IMPLEMENTATION SERVICES

Security implementation

Turn security requirements into controls and procedures your organisation can operate.

WHERE THIS HELPS

Move from planning to operation

Policies help when people can put them into practice. We connect agreed requirements to delivery tasks, operational ownership and supporting evidence.

THE WORK INVOLVED

A scope shaped around your requirements

01

Review the requirements

Establish controls in scope, current arrangements and dependencies that affect delivery.

02

Implement the agreed changes

Work with designated owners on procedures and control improvements within the agreed engagement.

03

Review progress and evidence

Keep implementation actions, decisions and supporting records connected at each review point.

04

Prepare operational ownership

Review remaining work and equip your team with documentation and responsibilities for handover.

PRACTICAL OUTPUTS

What the engagement can provide

Deliverables are confirmed with you before work begins.

  • A scoped control implementation plan
  • Practical policies and procedures
  • A record of progress and decisions
  • Reviewed evidence and handover actions

Agreeing the boundaries

Technical deployment, tooling, infrastructure changes and third-party costs are included only where expressly agreed in scope.
