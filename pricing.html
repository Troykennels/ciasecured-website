BEFORE WE START

A few things you may want to know

How is the fee worked out?+

The proposal reflects the agreed scope, deliverables, existing work and the people needed. No fixed subscription or package fee is shown before those details are understood.

Can we ask for help with just one part of the work?+

Yes. Describe the gap or task you need help with. Discovery establishes the boundaries and how the work fits into your current programme.

Are external assessor fees included?+

External assessment and certification fees are separate unless the proposal explicitly includes them.

What happens after we send our enquiry?+

We discuss the requirement with you, clarify the scope and agree whether a proposal is the right next step. The form does not book a meeting or commit you to a purchase.
