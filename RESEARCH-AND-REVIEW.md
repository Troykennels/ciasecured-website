# Website research and review — 3 November 2026

The existing Mycroft and Hypersence references remain part of the visual brief.
This review adds the following sources. Their branding, clients, certificates,
statistics and commercial claims have not been copied to CIASecured.

- BSI — https://www.bsigroup.com/ — clear service and standards routes informed
  the framework directory, related solutions and contextual enquiry links.
- LRQA — https://www.lrqa.com/en-us/ — separation of services, solutions and
  client access informed the navigation and the distinction between preparation
  support and independent assessment.
- Control Risks — https://www.controlrisks.com/ — direct service descriptions and
  visible routes to contact informed the service output sections and enquiry flow.
- GOV.UK Design System — https://design-system.service.gov.uk/styles/type-scale/
  — a shared relative type scale informed consistent headings, body text,
  supporting text and controls across desktop and phone layouts.
- W3C WAI — https://www.w3.org/WAI/tutorials/carousels/animations/ — informed
  hover/focus suspension and reduced-motion handling. The owner specifically
  requested removal of visible play/pause and arrow controls; these are omitted.
- Nielsen Norman Group — https://www.nngroup.com/articles/auto-forwarding/
  — informed the slower rotation and keeping service links available outside
  the rotating hero.

## Changes applied

Consistent typography across every page; no website UI arrows; no visible
pause/play controls; rotation suspended on hover, keyboard interaction, hidden
tabs and out-of-view hero. Rotation resumes when those conditions clear, unless
the visitor requests reduced motion. Manual slide selection and swipe remain.

Services describe specific outputs. Framework pages include questions to discuss
and related areas. Pricing and the homepage answer practical questions. Search
finds services and framework pages without sending the query to an external service.
Enquiries remember the selected service or framework, then offer reviewed email,
WhatsApp and clipboard options. Nothing is automatically sent to anyone.

Website information explains the form behaviour. A custom page-not-found route
helps visitors recover from broken links. The real client application remains
separate, and its screenshots retain their original interface content.
